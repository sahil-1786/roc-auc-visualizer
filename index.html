How to interpret what you see

  • Threshold lower karo -> zyada observations positive classify hongi -> usually Recall/TPR up, but FPR bhi up ho sakta hai.
  • Threshold higher karo -> model strict ho jaata hai -> usually FPR down, but Recall down ho sakta hai.
  • ROC curve top-left ke closer ho to model better separate kar raha hai.
  • AUC high ho to overall ranking quality achhi hoti hai, even agar koi ek specific threshold poor ho.
